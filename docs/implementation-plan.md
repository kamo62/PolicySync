## Architecture and invariants

Required P0 shape: Python 3.12+, FastAPI modular monolith, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy 2.x and Alembic, SQLite on one local machine, Jinja2/HTMX and accessible CSS, private local evidence storage. Use service functions for domain rules without HTTP imports, per-request sessions, HTML and `/api/v1` routes, and a narrow optional Lark adapter. A local worker is optional. No Docker, PostgreSQL, Lark, LLM key, OCR or external API is required for the seeded journey. Default bind is `127.0.0.1`; local-only unauthenticated endpoints must not be exposed to a network. Development OpenAPI is local only and `/healthz` reports DB/schema health without sensitive detail.

- **Persistence:** UUID strings, UTC timestamps, explicit booleans, money as integer minor units plus ISO currency, portable JSON, foreign keys and optimistic versions. Enable SQLite foreign keys on every connection, WAL and a busy timeout; keep transactions short. Alembic owns schema changes, with safe downgrades and data-preservation checks. SQLite does not provide pilot isolation, high availability or network-share safety. PostgreSQL compatibility is a P1 test and migration path, not a P0 dependency.
- **Evidence:** stable evidence item with immutable, separately numbered versions, bytes and SHA-256; preserve origin, author/sender, receipt time, MIME and classifications. Private storage is outside static paths. Validate size, type and paths; atomically write and reconcile file and DB failures, with safe logged orphan cleanup. PDF needs page references; spans/paragraphs and excerpts identify other sources. Verify exact extracted text where possible, but semantic support needs a human. Scanned or ambiguous OCR requires manual verification or stays unresolved. Multiple classification flags may coexist; deny external inclusion unless explicitly allowed and safe.
- **Assertions:** `proposed → under_review → accepted/rejected/disputed`; accepted material requires a valid same-matter source version and precise anchor, reviewer confirmation of support and context, actor, time and reason. A citation alone is not proof. Wrong pages, unsupported claims, ambiguous OCR and unresolved contradictions block acceptance as fact; show disputes and gaps as such. An amendment creates a traceable revision. Version replacement moves affected accepted assertions to `needs_revalidation`; competing dates and policy references remain distinct, with no automatic coverage, claims-made/occurrence or legal-date determination.
- **Artefacts and invalidation:** `draft → review_pending → approved → published`, with changes requested returning to draft. Approval is for the exact content, audience, template and pinned inputs; publication records release actor, versions and hash. Any material change after approval requires fresh review. Evidence replacement invalidates affected accepted assertions and dependent draft packs, including an already approved but unpublished report; publication must recheck support, current evidence, classification and approval at the point of release. **Proposed:** record explicit input/version dependencies and an invalidation reason; mark existing published artefacts as superseded or requiring attention without changing their immutable bytes, and require a new reviewed version for further release. The precise treatment of already published reports and handovers needs agreement below. A failed check must not silently reuse an older approved draft.
- **Privilege:** insurer output is built from an explicit audience allow-list, not a page dump. Exclude internal-only, privileged and sensitive material unless explicitly safe under an agreed policy; classification changes trigger re-evaluation of dependent reports. Check derived excerpts, titles, references, issue commentary, attachments, metadata and error messages as well as document bodies. Internal audit ZIP and external report are separate audiences: the local audit pack can contain authorised internal lineage, while no external template/export may leak privileged material. P0 has no secure user isolation; this distinction is a content control, not a claim of access security.
- **Workflow:** matter transitions record actor and reason; closure preserves history. Instructions distinguish draft, issued, delivery confirmed, acknowledged, responded and completed; failed delivery and overdue are independent alerts. P0 issued events are explicitly simulated and never proof of actual dispatch or receipt. Handover `draft → submitted → acknowledged` captures receiver, uncertainties and caveats; acknowledgement resolves neither issues nor authority. Record critical-date type, basis, source and reviewer; legal dates stay provisional until qualified human confirmation.
- **Audit and concurrency:** record append-only business events for material edits, decisions, transitions and exports, with actor, time, target and before/after hashes; chain hashes detect simple tampering but do not provide WORM protection against a privileged DB operator. Reject stale optimistic versions, including review/publication races, and commit state, approval and event consistently. Avoid sensitive content in logs and metrics.
- **CRM:** `CRM_PROVIDER=none` by default; claims remain usable offline. Only manually initiated Lark organisation-to-PolicySync mapping and non-sensitive engagement fields are in the initial contract, with explicit stable external IDs and field allow-list. No claim IDs, identities, policy data, evidence, privilege, medical information or reserves cross the boundary. Lark is not authoritative for claim tasks or approval. Stub is sufficient for offline P0; a live adapter is optional and needs current API, permissions and terms checked before use.

## Decisions and source ambiguities for discussion

1. **Source availability:** obtain v1.4, the October addendum and business plan; check for controlling requirements absent from the consolidated summary. Appoint the accountable claims SME and validate synthetic scenarios and authority boundaries.
2. **Input formats:** §§5 and 6 imply PDF/DOCX/EML/TXT; §11 makes DOCX conditional and first run text-extractable PDF/TXT. Proposed P0 minimum is TXT, extractable PDF and EML, with DOCX only if safe deterministic processing is agreed. Confirm whether DOCX is a release gate and define upload limits and manual span conventions.
3. **Imports:** §1 mentions manual read-only CSV/file import, while FR-19 places controlled source-system imports in P1. Decide whether P0 needs a synthetic manual CSV importer or only manual evidence/seed input; do not infer an authorised insurer integration.
4. **Authorisation in demo:** §5 asks for a separate authoriser and §3 permits a single demo account. Proposed P0 records distinct simulated roles/actors and a release decision without claiming identity security; confirm demonstration separation and who may approve own material.
5. **Classification and external gaps:** §5 lists three labels; §11 permits multiple flags including `sensitive_personal`. Confirm combinations, safe redaction and which unresolved issue summaries may appear externally without leaking protected information.
6. **Invalidation scope:** confirm which changes to evidence, classifications, assertions, issue/task content, recipients or templates invalidate approval; agree how to flag already published reports and acknowledged handovers, preserve historical snapshots and communicate corrections. No automatic external recall or sending is assumed.
7. **Anchors and conflicts:** agree minimum anchors for EML/TXT, handling of unextractable passages, the meaning of disputed versus accepted material, and how a qualified reviewer records resolution without silently choosing a policy or legal outcome.
8. **CRM:** decide whether a live Lark adapter is wanted in P0 or stub/link only. Before live use verify current API/field versions, tier limits, region, permissions, terms and credentials handling; initial data direction remains manually initiated and allow-listed.
9. **Seed-data guard:** “production-looking” is not precisely defined; agree a practical synthetic-only fixture convention and review process. A heuristic cannot prove data is synthetic.
10. **Evidence and audit export:** confirm audit-pack access, included draft/metadata and personal-field sanitisation, and whether customer portability requires originals as well as the specified index; P0 internal audit and external insurer outputs need different handling.
11. **Metrics and pilot:** define timing start/stop, correction/error definitions, participant consent and baseline protocol with the design partner; no buyer commitment or measured benefit is assumed.
12. **P1 specifics:** choose customer isolation, identity, key management, retention/hold, privilege ownership, authorised import and dispatch scope only with the customer and relevant specialists. Review medical malpractice variations with a clinician/claims SME. Licensing, POPIA and insurer permissions remain open assessments, not conclusions.
